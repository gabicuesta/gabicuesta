**CTO · Business-minded technologist · Madrid, Spain**

For more than ten years I've been turning business vision into technical architecture, taking products from first prototype to large-scale operation and building the teams that run them.

I believe technology should be **invisible to users, profitable for the business, and predictable for the team**. Today most of my energy goes into the same question every engineering leader is facing: how to bring AI into real products and real teams without losing quality, cost control or trust.

#### What I'm working on

| Project | What it explores |

#### How I think about AI in engineering

- **Evals before opinions.** If you can't measure it, you can't ship it.
- **Cost and latency are product features**, not afterthoughts.
- **Agents augment teams.** The goal is better engineers, not fewer standards.
- **Boring architecture, sharp edges only where they pay off.**

**Cómo entiendo la IA en ingeniería**

- **Primero evals, luego opiniones.** Lo que no se mide no se despliega.
- **El coste y la latencia son parte del producto.**
- **Los agentes potencian a los equipos.** El objetivo son mejores ingenieros, no estándares más bajos.
- **Arquitectura aburrida; innovación solo donde compensa.**
